VINLAND SAGA — CINZEL
Innioasis Y1 theme.

Files:

The theme uses a muted Nordic palette: charcoal, weathered green, stone gray,
aged parchment, and muted ochre.

Upload the ZIP through the Innioasis theme uploader, or extract the folder
into /storage/sdcard0/Themes/ on the Y1.
