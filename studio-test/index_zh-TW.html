關於 Innioasis Y1 & Y2 佈景主題工作室

Innioasis Theme Studio is the official browser-based theme maker, theme editor, and skin creator for Innioasis Y1 和 Innioasis Y2 players running Original OS, Better-Y, Solar, or Koensayr.

  • Full Y1 & Y2 support: Edit Home icons (including the Y2 File Manager icon), Y2 categorized Settings icons, item backgrounds, right arrows, wallpapers, masks, custom fonts, and menu colors.
  • 1:1 live firmware simulator: Switch between Both, Y2, and Y1 layouts with authentic Android .9.png nine-patch rendering and click-to-edit selection.
  • Try on Device & publish: Export a ready-to-install ZIP, copy directly to your player over USB, or publish straight to the Innioasis 佈景主題畫廊.

USB 儲存模式

Try this theme on your player

Copy your current Studio theme straight into your player’s Themes folder over USB so you can test it on real hardware right away.

Testing again in this session?

After ejecting USB storage, restart your device 和 select another theme (then switch back) so your player clears its cache and loads your updated files.

  1. 1
    Enable USB Storage Mode on your player

    Connect your Innioasis Y1 or Y2 over USB and enable USB 儲存模式 on the player so its storage appears on your computer.

  2. 2
    Click “Select Themes folder”

    Choose the Themes folder on your player. Studio will copy your theme directly into it for testing.

Direct USB folder access isn’t available in this browser

Picking your player’s Themes folder directly requires a Chromium desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera) over HTTPS. You can still save the theme ZIP to your device and copy the unzipped folder into your player’s Themes 資料夾。
