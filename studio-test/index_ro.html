Despre Innioasis Y1 & Y2 Theme Studio

Innioasis Theme Studio is the official browser-based theme maker, theme editor, and skin creator for Innioasis Y1 şi Innioasis Y2 players running Original OS, Better-Y, Solar, or Koensayr.

  • Full Y1 & Y2 support: Edit Home icons (including the Y2 File Manager icon), Y2 categorized Settings icons, item backgrounds, right arrows, wallpapers, masks, custom fonts, and menu colors.
  • 1:1 live firmware simulator: Switch between Both, Y2, and Y1 layouts with authentic Android .9.png nine-patch rendering and click-to-edit selection.
  • Try on Device & publish: Export a ready-to-install ZIP, copy directly to your player over USB, or publish straight to the Galeria de teme vizuale Innioasis.

Getting started

Welcome to Innioasis Theme Studio

Design, remix, preview, and publish custom themes for your Innioasis Y1 or Y2 right in your browser — no desktop software required.

Start from a gallery theme as a template

Pick any existing theme from the community gallery to remix its icons, wallpapers, masks, fonts, and colors into something new.

Drag & drop or browse a local folder or ZIP

Drop a theme folder or .zip anywhere on the website — or open one from your device — to inspect and edit it locally.

Save for your player or upload to the gallery

Export a ready-to-install ZIP to your device anytime, or switch to the Încărcați tab to share your creation (or update your listing) with the community.

USB Mod stocare

Try this theme on your player

Copy your current Studio theme straight into your player’s Themes folder over USB so you can test it on real hardware right away.

Testing again in this session?

After ejecting USB storage, restart your device şi select another theme (then switch back) so your player clears its cache and loads your updated files.

  1. 1
    Enable USB Storage Mode on your player

    Connect your Innioasis Y1 or Y2 over USB and enable USB Mod stocare on the player so its storage appears on your computer.

  2. 2
    Click “Select Themes folder”

    Choose the Themes folder on your player. Studio will copy your theme directly into it for testing.

Direct USB folder access isn’t available in this browser

Picking your player’s Themes folder directly requires a Chromium desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera) over HTTPS. You can still save the theme ZIP to your device and copy the unzipped folder into your player’s Themes pliant.
