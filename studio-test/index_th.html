เกี่ยวกับ Innioasis Y1 & Y2 Theme Studio

Innioasis Theme Studio is the official browser-based theme maker, theme editor, and skin creator for Innioasis Y1 และ Innioasis Y2 players running Original OS, Better-Y, Solar, or Koensayr.

  • Full Y1 & Y2 support: Edit Home icons (including the Y2 File Manager icon), Y2 categorized Settings icons, item backgrounds, right arrows, wallpapers, masks, custom fonts, and menu colors.
  • 1:1 live firmware simulator: Switch between Both, Y2, and Y1 layouts with authentic Android .9.png nine-patch rendering and click-to-edit selection.
  • Try on Device & publish: Export a ready-to-install ZIP, copy directly to your player over USB, or publish straight to the แกลเลอรีธีมและสกิน Innioasis.

Testing again in this session?

After ejecting USB storage, restart your device และ select another theme (then switch back) so your player clears its cache and loads your updated files.

  1. 1
    Enable USB Storage Mode on your player

    Connect your Innioasis Y1 or Y2 over USB and enable USB โหมดการจัดเก็บ on the player so its storage appears on your computer.

  2. 2
    Click “Select Themes folder”

    Choose the Themes folder on your player. Studio will copy your theme directly into it for testing.
