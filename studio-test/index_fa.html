Getting started

Welcome to Innioasis Theme Studio

Design, remix, preview, and publish custom themes for your Innioasis Y1 or Y2 right in your browser — no desktop software required.

Start from a gallery theme as a template

Drag & drop or browse a local folder or ZIP

Drop a theme folder or .zip anywhere on the website — or open one from your device — to inspect and edit it locally.

Copy your current Studio theme straight into your player’s Themes folder over USB so you can test it on real hardware right away.

Testing again in this session?

After ejecting USB storage, restart your device و select another theme (then switch back) so your player clears its cache and loads your updated files.

  1. 1
    Enable USB Storage Mode on your player

    Connect your Innioasis Y1 or Y2 over USB and enable USB حالت ذخیره سازی on the player so its storage appears on your computer.

  2. 2
    Click “Select Themes folder”

    Choose the Themes folder on your player. Studio will copy your theme directly into it for testing.
