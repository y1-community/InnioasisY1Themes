Timmkoo and other Innioasis players

The themes here are for the Innioasis Y1 and the Innioasis Y2. They do not apply to the Timmkoo players or the other Innioasis models.

What to use instead

System updates for those models are in the firmware archive, installed with Updater CE. The model printed on the player has to match the package.

  • Timmkoo and other Innioasis firmware
  • Innioasis Y1 firmware
  • Innioasis Y2 firmware

If you have a Y1 or Y2, the themes you want are in the gallery.
