# Innioasis Y2 Firmware 3.2.0

*Build date 13 August 2026 · Published 13 September 2026 · Android 4.4.4 · Y2 hardware*

The biggest Y2 update yet. FM radio comes to the Y2, Settings is rebuilt into clear categories, and the sound and click wheel finally become adjustable.

## What's in this update?

- **FM radio on the Y2.** FM Radio returns to the home menu on FM-equipped Y2 units, with the familiar channel list and favourites.
- **Settings, organised.** The long flat list is replaced by six categories — **Keys & Wheel**, **Audio**, **Display**, **Personalization**, **Files & Storage** and **System** — with Bluetooth, About and Factory reset kept at the top level. Bluetooth also moves from the home screen into Settings, freeing a home slot.
- **Turn the speaker off.** A new Audio → Speaker toggle enables or disables the built-in speaker, which is handy when you only ever listen on headphones or line out.
- **Headphone gain.** Audio → Headphone Gain offers four output levels — Low, Standard, High and Maximum — so quiet IEMs and harder-to-drive headphones can both be matched properly.
- **Click wheel control.** Keys & Wheel adds Click Wheel Sensitivity (Low, Standard, High) and Adjust Volume with Click Wheel (on or off), so the wheel can stop changing volume if you keep brushing it. Screen-off media mode now accepts the centre button as well.
- **Album art style.** Music → Settings → Album Art Style switches the now-playing cover between **Perspective** (tilted with a glossy reflection) and **Flat** (square with a reflection).
- **Video scanning you control.** Instead of deleting scan paths by hand, Video Settings now offers Auto Scan Common Folders, Scan Specified Folders Only, or Scan Entire TF Card.
- **Pick up where you left off.** Music and video both ask whether to continue playing from where you stopped.

## For theme creators

- Themes can now set `showNativeIcon`, which decides whether the system icon is drawn when a theme does not supply one. All five bundled themes enable it; omit the key and it stays off.
- Settings theming grows from 50 to 67 slots: six category icons, four headphone gain icons, two speaker icons, three wheel sensitivity icons and two wheel volume icons.
- **Bluetooth has no separate Settings slot.** The Settings → Bluetooth icon is drawn from the home screen Bluetooth entry, so themes that blank the home icon blank the settings icon too.

## Good to know

- Categorized Settings, the speaker toggle, headphone gain and the wheel options all arrive here, not in 3.2.8.
- FM radio only appears on units with FM hardware.
