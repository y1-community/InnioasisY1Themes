# Innioasis Y1 Firmware 2.8.2

*Build date 31 July 2025 · Published 28 October 2025 · Android 4.2.2 · Type A Y1 hardware*

Books, a launcher switcher and a cleaner home screen. 2.8.2 is the update that adds the e-book reader engine and lets you hand the device over to an alternative launcher such as Rockbox.

## What's in this update?

- **An e-book reader arrives.** EPUB, PDF, TXT and Word documents can now be opened on the Y1, with a bookshelf library, saved reading progress, bookmarks, and font-size and reading-theme controls.
- **Launcher selector.** A new Launcher entry in Settings lets you switch between the stock interface and an alternative launcher you have installed, such as Rockbox — and switch back again without reflashing.
- **Home screen cleans up.** The Shuffle shortcut leaves the home menu, leaving eight focused icons: Now Playing, Music, Video, Audiobooks, Photos, FM Radio, Bluetooth and Settings. Older themes stay compatible, because the saved Shuffle setting is still read and simply ignored.
- **Smoother music lists.** Long file names now scroll instead of being cut off, so you can finally tell two similarly named tracks apart.
- **Reliability fixes.** Video playback no longer keeps running after you pause it, the player no longer fails with an error at startup in certain library states, and the video scan is tidier so stray folders stop showing up as empty entries.

## For theme creators

2.8.2 adds three home screen slots — E-Book, Calculator and Calendar — and a Launcher toggle in Settings. Themes written for 2.1.9 keep working unchanged; themes written for this build can use the new slots.

## Good to know

- **Calculator and Calendar are included but hidden.** Both apps exist in this build with their home-screen slots, but retail units ship with them disabled, so most people will never see them.
- As with every Type A release, this cannot be installed on Type B scroll-wheel hardware.
