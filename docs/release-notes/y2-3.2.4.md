# Innioasis Y2 Firmware 3.2.4

*Build date 19 August 2026 · Published 13 November 2026 · Android 4.4.4 · Y2 hardware — retail shipping build*

The firmware most Y2 units shipped with, and the one most people will be upgrading *from*. It is 3.2.0 with retail polish rather than a new feature release.

## What's in this update?

- **About screen tidied.** The version line is shortened from `System Version:` to simply `Version:`, which makes the screen easier to read and easier to quote when asking for support.
- **Retail reliability work.** Performance and stability fixes for the shipping build, so the player behaves consistently out of the box.
- **Everything from 3.2.0 remains:** FM radio, categorized Settings, speaker toggle, headphone gain, click wheel sensitivity and volume, album art style, video scan modes, continue-playing prompts and the full 3.1.7 music, video and file manager features.

## For theme creators

The theme format is unchanged from 3.2.0. Themes built for 3.2.0 work exactly the same here, and the bundled themes are identical.

## Good to know

- The **ROM version is 3.2.4** while the launcher inside it reports **3.2.3**, so both numbers are correct — check About to see which you have.
- Devices on this build show `收音机` as the launcher name in some places; that is fixed in 3.2.8.
- Upgrading to 3.2.8 is safe and keeps your themes and settings.
