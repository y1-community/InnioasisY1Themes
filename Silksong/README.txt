NOTES

- (IMPORTANT) Contained is a folder of wallpapers, you can swap these out if you want by dragging the folder into the photos directory of your player, and then changing them in settings.
- NOT FINAL VERSION! Will be updated to fix issues and for the new DLC releasing 2026 (hopefully. look, you can't have any silksong related thing without doubt of the release date.)
- Feel free to switch out the font, Cinzel probably gets annoying to look at after a while.
- The EQ icons were meant to follow the music genre, but really the only ones that match are Classical and Rock.
- This is my first theme! I probably got some stuff wrong, so feel free to lmk any tips or improvements for the theme.
- This is also not well optimized. I barely resized any images during the process.
- Also, yes, the screenshots were taken in the editor on the themes page. I couldn't get the screenshot feature working on the updater.
