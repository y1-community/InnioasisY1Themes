How to install baja blast ocean on Innioasis Y1 & Y2

  1. Click Download theme above to save the ready-to-install ZIP, or click Preview in Studio and choose Try on Device to copy it directly over USB.
  2. Connect your Innioasis Y1 or Y2 to your computer, enable USB Storage Mode on the player, and copy the unzipped baja_blast_ocean folder into the player’s Themes folder.
  3. Safely eject USB storage, then open Settings → Change Theme on your player and select baja blast ocean. See the full theme installation guide for tips.
