# Testers needed: Solar for Y1

 Solar Enables Wi-Fi on your Y1 and unlocks a world of modern capabilities on your Innioasis Y1 — from direct on-device theme downloads and ad-free streaming to visual Cover Flow browsing. ## What Solar Brings to the Y1

 Solar is a full interface replacement and modern operating system for the Innioasis Y1 that enables Wi-Fi and direct on-device network tools. 

### Direct Theme Downloads

Download and apply themes from this gallery right onto your Y1 over Wi-Fi. No PC, browser, or USB transfer required.

### Ad-Free YouTube

Stream or download offline YouTube videos and high-quality audio tracks directly on your player, completely without ads.

### Reach (Deezer & Soulseek)

Search, play, and download music directly over Wi-Fi using your Deezer session (MP3/FLAC without ads) or Soulseek P2P sharing.

### Flow Album Browsing

Experience smooth Cover Flow-style visual album navigation directly on the device for an intuitive, retro music feel.

### Podcasts & Navidrome

Search and download favorite podcast episodes over the internet, or connect to your own personal self-hosted Navidrome library.

### Over-The-Air Updates

Receive and install future Solar updates directly over Wi-Fi right from the device settings without plugging into a PC.

### Global Quick Menu

Access a global context menu from any screen to control playback, queue management, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth settings instantly.

### Full Theme Compatibility

100% compatible with all community themes from this site. Keep your custom looks while unlocking modern OS features.

## How to Install Solar

 Installing Solar on your Innioasis Y1 is quick and easy right inside the official Innioasis Updater application. 
  - 1Open Innioasis Updater

Launch the official Innioasis Updater tool on your computer
  - 2Select Solar from the Software Dropdown

Pick Solar directly from the Perisian dropdown list within Updater.
  - 3Click Install

Hit the Pasang button. The updater will download the firmware and flash Solar to your device automatically.
Note: Solar is currently in alpha testing and [@TheSolarProject needs your feedback](https://github.com/thesolarproject/solar/issues). Once installed, future updates can be downloaded and applied right over Wi-Fi without needing your computer. Y2 support is coming soon.

### Alternative Installation Methods

Manual zip via Updater: Download the latest `rom.zip` daripada [Solar GitHub Releases](https://github.com/thesolarproject/solar/releases/latest) page, click Browse Files in Innioasis Updater, and select the downloaded zip file to begin installation. 

 Advanced users can also flash Solar using SP Flash Tool 5.1924 atau MTKClient. For complete flashing instructions and developer documentation, visit the [Solar project documentation](https://github.com/thesolarproject/solar#readme-ov-file).
