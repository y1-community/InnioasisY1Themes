# Privacy - Innioasis Themes Gallery

# Privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026

themes.innioasis.app is a community theme gallery for the Innioasis Y1 and Y2. It is run by Ryan Specter with the Innioasis community. Luci Ltd provides hosting. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

## What stays on your device

The gallery saves a few settings in your browser: your language, colour theme, text size, privacy choices, whether you hid the donation appeals, and translations it has already fetched. These stay on your device. Clearing site data removes them.

## What we measure

We use Google Tag Manager with Google Analytics to count page views and to see which pages fail or load slowly. Cloudflare provides hosting, security and attack protection. The gallery also keeps its own totals for theme views, downloads and ratings, and stores a random visitor id in your browser so a refresh is not counted twice. Public theme totals do not identify you.

We do not ask for your name, email or postal address, and we do not sell data.

## Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser storage for the settings above and for aggregate counting. Google Analytics and Cloudflare may set their own cookies. You can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time; the gallery will still work.

## Your choices

You can turn off analytics and rating contributions without losing access to the gallery. The setting is remembered on this device and shared with the firmware archive and Updater CE through a cookie on innioasis.app.

Analytics is currently on for this browser.

Turn analytics off

## Donations

Donation buttons link to Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal, Revolut, Patreon and Boosty. Those services handle the payment and their own data. This site never sees your card details.

## Links to other sites

Theme pages link to authors' own sites, and the footer links to Discord, Reddit and GitHub. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We do not control what they collect.

## Your rights

If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can ask what personal data relates to you, ask for a copy, ask us to delete it, or object to processing. California residents can ask to know or delete personal information and can opt out of sale; we do not sell it.

Because the gallery has no accounts, we may not be able to connect a request to a visitor without the random id stored in that visitor's browser. You can contact us through the [repository issues page](https://github.com/y1-community/InnioasisY1Themes/issues) or [r/innioasis](https://reddit.com/r/innioasis).

## Children

The gallery is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted on the gallery home page.

The English text on this page is the authoritative version. Translated copies are provided so the policy is readable in your language; if a translation and the English text disagree, the English text applies.
