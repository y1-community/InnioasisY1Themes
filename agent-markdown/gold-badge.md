## How we decide

 Gold is a community quality mark, not a warranty. We automatically check each theme so the badge matches what is in the package. If a future Y1 or Y2 update breaks something, the badge can come off until it is fixed. Technical details (for theme makers)

A theme earns Gold when it passes our artwork compatibility check (same as the gallery badge):
  - `config.json` lists paths for the Rockbox launcher switch, eBook reader, calculator, and calendar.
  - Each linked file exists and is real artwork - not `transparent.png` or `backfill.png` placeholders on `launcher.png`, `calendar.png`, `ebook.png`, or `calculator.png`.
  - Authors may set `gallery.compatibilityMedal` to `gold` or `none` to force the badge on or off.

 Themes are checked in batches when you open this page or the gallery. Brand-new uploads may take a moment before Gold appears. 

 Always back up your Y1 or Y2 before installing any theme.
